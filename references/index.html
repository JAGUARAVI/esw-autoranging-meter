Measure capacitors
from picofarads to hundreds
of microfarads, automatically.

One ESP32-S3 drives two measurement physics: an LM393 hardware-in-the-loop relaxation oscillator for small ceramics, and ADC RC step-response tracking for large electrolytics. A PhotoMOS safety bank and a 1000 µF blocking capacitor let you inject 0–20 V DC bias without destroying the MCU pins. Every (range, method) sample is scored and fused into one high-confidence reading.

05 · The fusion engine

Every (range, method) pair is a sensor

The firmware does not pick one range and one method. It collects many plausible samples, scores each with a physics-based quality metric, gates outliers against the median, and fuses what survives into one estimate.
